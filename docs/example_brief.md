## Failure pattern (2-3 sentences)
Owners of 2011 Hyundai Sonata vehicles report engine failures characterized by sudden stalling at highway speeds, abnormal engine noises, rapid oil consumption, and engine seizure. In several instances, sudden loss of power or engine restarts were accompanied by smoke emissions and fires originating in the engine compartment. Drivers report that these failures often occur unexpectedly without prior warning indicators illuminating on the dashboard.
